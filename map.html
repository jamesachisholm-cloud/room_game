How to edit the map

Open maps/world.map. Each grid cell is a 7×7 bay. Repeating a room number joins those bays into one room; use connected cells to make larger or L-shaped rooms.

Internal door lines use room IDs: door 01 05 3 connects Rooms 01 and 05, three map units along their shared wall. The offset starts at the west end of a horizontal wall or the north end of a vertical wall. Remove a line to close that doorway. A room pair must share one continuous wall.

exit east 07 places an outside door on the east exterior wall of room 07, centered along that room's boundary. The connections section links exits and paths.

Object lines are box col row x y width depth height or cat col row x y scale; x and y are map units within the bay. This page is the visual map reference, so save the source and refresh the preview after editing.
