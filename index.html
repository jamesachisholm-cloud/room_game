    ROOM LINES

    Explore five wireframe buildings, including a central hub with four paths to the ring. Building A has 11 rooms, Building B has 7, Building C has 8, and Building D has 9. Buildings A–D connect in a ring with long paths. A central one-room hub has four doors and paths that meet the ring paths at T junctions.

    Move with W A S D or the arrow keys. Turn with your mouse. Press E near an object to pick it up. Carry up to 10 objects; press 1–9 or 0 to select a slot, then G to drop it. Drop an object in a room with its matching picture to earn credits; pick it up again to reverse the credit.

    On a touch screen: use the arrow buttons at the bottom right (up and down move, left and right turn), tap PICK UP or DROP at the bottom left, and tap an inventory slot to select it.
