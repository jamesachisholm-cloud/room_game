    Escape from Tzargarrot

    Explore the buildings. Earn enough credits to escape.

    Move with W A S D or the arrow keys. Turn with the left and right arrow keys. Press E near an object to pick it up. Press 1–9 or 0 to select a slot, then G to drop it. Drop an object in a room with its matching picture to earn credits.

    On a touch screen: tap the view above its lower quarter to walk forward (the higher the tap, the farther) and tap below that line to step back, or use the arrow buttons at the bottom right (up and down move, left and right turn). Tap PICK UP or DROP at the bottom left, and tap an inventory slot to select it.
